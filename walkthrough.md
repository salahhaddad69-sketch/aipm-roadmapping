# Roadmap Walkthrough

**Audience:** Head of Supply Chain and CFO (leadership)
**Length:** about 5–7 minutes
**Present from:** `roadmap.md` as the visual anchor; other files on demand.

---

## 1. Vision, Strategy, and Objectives
*(Show: `strategy-brief.md`)*

Our vision: inventory managers and finance can **see future stock risks early**
and act before stockouts or overstock happen.

IBP improved our strategic and tactical planning, but it did not improve
inventory management enough. That gap between the plan and the stock is our
focus.

We have three objectives:

- **Obj 1:** reduce **both** stockouts and excess stock.
- **Obj 2:** give finance visibility of inventory value and risk.
- **Obj 3:** make the data reliable enough for predictions.

We have **no baselines yet**, so we are not promising any numbers today.

## 2. Strongest Evidence and Its Limits
*(Show: `evidence-map.md`)*

Our strongest evidence is practitioner experience from the IBP project:

- Safety stock and reorder points were still set **manually** or rarely
  updated.
- **Promotions and seasonal peaks** caused stock problems even when the
  forecast was acceptable.
- **Data quality and integration** were the main difficulties.

**Limits, said plainly:** this comes from one person, recalled from memory,
with no figures. A vendor page (o9) claims large improvements from AI, but those
are unverified marketing claims, and **we do not use them as targets**.
**The finance need is still an assumption.**

## 3. Roadmap Format and Horizons
*(Show: `roadmap.md`, Horizon Definitions)*

We chose **Now-Next-Later** because uncertainty is high and there are no fixed
deadlines. The columns are **confidence levels, not dates**:

- **Now:** we are actively working on it.
- **Next:** likely, but open questions remain.
- **Later:** an option we are watching.

"Next" does **not** mean "next quarter".

## 4. Initiatives and Exclusions
*(Show: `roadmap.md`, Roadmap table and Exclusions)*

| Now | Next | Later |
|---|---|---|
| I04: Make the data ready *(main effort)* | I02: Get stock ready for promotions and peaks *(paused)* | — |
| I01: Safety stock in line with demand *(learning only)* | | |
| I03: Ask finance whether they need an inventory risk view | | |

**What we will not do:**

- build an IBP system from scratch;
- place orders automatically (a person approves every recommendation);
- change supplier or production planning;
- roll out to everything at once (we start with one pilot).

**Whether we need AI at all is still open.** A simpler method, such as IBP's
built-in safety stock calculation, may be enough. We decide after learning.

## 5. Confidence, Dependencies, Gates, and Commitments

- **Confidence:** Low to Medium. All evidence so far comes from one source.
- **Dependencies:** everything depends on **usable data (I04)**.
- **Gates:** IT security and data access approval; a data quality gate before
  any prediction is used; a person approves every change.
- **What we commit to:** only the **learning work in Now**, subject to your
  capacity approval. The solution approach, I02, and anything built for finance
  remain **exploratory or optional**. There are **no fixed dates**.

## 6. The Change and How the Roadmap Responded
*(Show: `roadmap-review.md`)*

**Scenario (fictional):** the data check showed that the pilot's sales history
is too poor for prediction.

How we responded:

- **I04 adapted:** we check other product groups and switch the pilot if their
  data is better.
- **I01 adapted:** only the learning that needs no data continues; the
  approach decision is paused.
- **I02 paused.**
- **I03 advanced:** asking finance needs no data, so it moved from Later to
  Now.

All earlier versions are kept in the roadmap's change history.
