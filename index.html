Inventory Prediction Roadmap

How better inventory prediction, added to the existing ERP/IBP system, could reduce stockouts and excess stock. It shows what is known, what is uncertain, and what could change the direction. AI is one candidate approach; whether it is needed is decided after the learning steps.

Not on this roadmap

  • Developing an IBP system from scratchThe existing planning capability is kept and extended
  • Automatic purchase ordersThe AI or tool only suggests, and a person approves every order
  • Supplier and production planningOut of scope for now, to keep the focus on inventory
  • Full rollout to all plants or product groupsStart with one plant or product group, and learn before scaling
  • Using vendor figures (S02) as targetsThey are unverified marketing claims
